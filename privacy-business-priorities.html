Purpose And Information Used

The app helps authorized users review work already recorded in Jira, including workload by workspace, native status and due dates, accounting-service review order and selected-ticket comments. It does not create a separate collection form, payment instruction or taxpayer register.

It reads the current Jira viewer's identity, display name, active status, site context and time zone to verify the session. It requests visible workspaces and issue metadata: ticket reference and heading, workspace, status, priority, dates, responsible person, issue type, accounting provider and accounting service. Opening a visible ticket reads that ticket's details and available comments, including author names, comment dates, text and supporting URLs. Some response fields are used for verification or discarded rather than displayed.

Corporate and private-client tickets can contain personal, financial, tax or other confidential information. The app shows only information returned under the current viewer's Jira rights. Underlying records were supplied in Jira; their collection purposes and legal basis belong to the responsible source organization and actual client relationship. This notice describes the report's use of those records for internal work review.

Access And Data Flow

Requests run as the user interacting with the report through Atlassian's Jira browser bridge. The app's read scopes do not grant additional workspace, issue or comment rights. Workspace and provider selections narrow displayed results; they do not grant access. The app does not use an owner's reporting token or shared private extract for another user's view.

Jira and Forge deliver the report to the viewer's browser. The current app has no app-operated backend, database, Forge storage, external analytics or AI connection. Fonts, charts and branding are bundled. It does not send tickets or questions to a separate app-operated service, sell data or use it for advertising.

The GHK filter selects already visible accounting work with the configured provider mapping. It does not invite recipients or send a partner report. Original Jira and SharePoint links retain the destination's own permissions. The app does not download attachment files or SharePoint document contents.

Memory, Retention And Platform Processing

Report results remain in the current page's memory; the app writes no results, comments or questions to browser local/session storage. Refresh clears displayed results before rereading Jira. The page clears report output when hidden or left, after a detected identity change, or when access, identity or source checks fail. Changing filters clears ticket detail and comments; accessible source rows remain in memory for filtering during that session.

Users can refresh on demand or every 15 or 30 minutes while the page is visible. The app captures nothing on a background schedule after the report closes. Permission changes are checked on requests; instant revocation propagation is not guaranteed. Closing the page does not delete Jira records or independent viewer copies.

Authorized deployment, support and critical tests also produce private receipts, screenshots and review evidence retained by the operator. These review copies include user/account references and can include visible ticket information and comments. They are stored outside the app's page memory in the operator's private support files. Access and retention for that evidence follow the operator's actual support and review arrangements. The absence of an app database does not mean that the operator keeps no personal review information.

Underlying Jira records, platform logs and identity processing follow the applicable Jira customer and Forge arrangements. Atlassian's own controller processing is described in its Privacy Policy. Atlassian publishes supported hosting locations in Australia, Canada, Germany, Ireland, India, Japan, Singapore, South Korea, Switzerland, the United Kingdom and the United States. Its subprocessor list also identifies Sweden for certain hosting and describes other processing according to the service used. These are platform possibilities, not a claim that this Jira site's information is stored in every country listed.

For this report's Jira site, a native data residency read on 4 October 2026 showed Jira Service Management's location as “Not set” and its AWS region as “Europe (Frankfurt)”, Germany. It is not pinned to a selected residency location and may move across supported regions under Atlassian's arrangements. TriNova Business Priorities also showed its location as “Not set”. That app setting does not identify the exact country serving its static page or establish an app database. The native app's supported-location list additionally advertised Brazil as an option; eligibility does not establish current storage there. The report has no additional persistent report store.

Private deployment, support and test receipts and screenshots are retained as ordinary protected files on the service owner's Mac in Switzerland. The owner reports that these private support files are not synchronized to another store. Browser memory is on the viewer's device, whose location depends on that viewer. No Swiss-only or EU-only processing promise is made.

Insights And Your Requests

Insights are fixed rule-based messages evaluated in the page. Questions are not sent to a generative AI service. The report makes no automated recruitment, bonus, disciplinary, tax or payment decisions. Business importance, urgency and authority deadlines require validated source evidence; Jira status and due dates alone do not establish them.

Do not enter bank account numbers, passwords, API tokens or other secrets in Jira or the report. The app has no banking input, connector or payment action. Existing Jira text can still contain confidential information; this report is not a redaction service.

Contact milind@ms-ventures-group.com to route access, correction, deletion or other applicable privacy requests to the responsible organization. Use an appropriate private channel and avoid sending unnecessary ticket copies or credentials. The report cannot edit or delete source records. Rights, source retention and any representative or supervisory authority depend on the actual relationship and applicable law.
