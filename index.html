How We Work

Long-Term Capital, Institutional Discipline.

TriNova is built on committed, long-term private capital. Behind the group stand investors who fund in decades, not cycles — backing that lets TriNova steward a portfolio of more than 40 properties, in excess of EUR 50 million and growing, and carry development projects through their full life-cycle without dependence on the market's mood. That patience is deliberate: every project is financed with discipline, reported with precision, and taken to completion — the standard our banking partners expect, and receive.

That capital base is matched by an institutional way of working. From our Swiss Riviera base — with a competency centre in Hungary, dedicated near-shore capability hubs and a business-development presence in the Netherlands — TriNova originates and structures opportunities across the Benelux and the wider European institutional market: acquisitions, developments, joint ventures and management mandates, appraised, structured and negotiated with rigour, with every commitment decided and executed centrally by the group's management. We build long-term partnerships with institutional and private capital — pension funds, insurers, sovereign and family capital, private equity and credit providers — through joint ventures, club structures and programmatic partnerships; and we bring our hospitality and applied-AI practices into every mandate, pairing hotel and mixed-use expertise with data-driven market intelligence. Delivery follows the same design: a senior team and established independent specialists working to one Swiss standard — common methodology, playbooks and tools — so every engagement is consistent, whichever expert leads it.

Trust, Security & Compliance

Built On Trust, Held To A Higher Standard.

Creating value is only half the work — protecting it is the other. TriNova operates to the requirements of its home commune and canton, Montreux and the Canton de Vaud, and to Swiss federal law; and where European rules set a stricter bar, we apply the higher standard rather than the minimum.

01

Vaud & Montreux Compliance

Operated in line with Canton de Vaud commercial and trade requirements and the expectations of the Commune of Montreux — a genuine Swiss Riviera presence, represented accurately.

02

Swiss Federal Standards

Transparent, accountable data handling under the revised Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (revFADP), with clear legal‑entity identification and defined retention.

03

EU‑Grade Data Protection

For European clients and partners we handle personal data in line with the EU GDPR where it applies — lawful basis, transparency and data‑subject rights — and adopt whichever framework, Swiss or EU, sets the higher bar.

04

Security By Design

Encryption in transit, least‑privilege access, data minimisation and defined retention and deletion — so the information owners and guests entrust to us is safeguarded end to end.

TriNova AI & Advisory

Applied AI, From The Boardroom To Production.

We are data-driven, automated and digital-first by necessity — it is how we run premium operations at a Swiss standard. We built that capability for ourselves first, automating the work behind property, finance and hospitality, and we now bring it to Swiss and international enterprises and mid-market firms as a full AI & Advisory practice: we advise at board and C-suite level, and then we build the thing.

The practice is strongest where the work is document-heavy and regulated — M&A carve-outs and TSA management, contract operations, and GxP/GDPR-compliant pharma and finance workflows. Engagements are senior and deliberately short: board and operating-model advisory, executive workshops and AI hackathons, fixed-scope Agentic AI Opportunity Assessments, and production builds — AI agents, agent orchestration, connectors into your SAP, Salesforce and Veeva estate, and custom AI-enabled software — each proven in-house at TriNova before we deliver it to you.
